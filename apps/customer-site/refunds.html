Cancellations & Refunds

How to cancel an order, report a problem with your items and get your money back.

1. Cancelling an order

  • Before your order is packed or out for delivery: you can cancel it yourself in the Blynk app.
  • After it has been dispatched: you can no longer cancel in the app. Contact us by phone or WhatsApp as soon as possible and we will help where we can. Perishable items that have already left the store may not be refundable unless there is a problem with them.
  • We may cancel an order ourselves, for example if items are unavailable, your address cannot be served, payment is not confirmed, or online payment is not completed within the time shown in the app.

2. Orders we cancel or cannot fulfil

If you paid for an order that was cancelled (by you before dispatch, or by us) or that we could not fulfil, you will receive a full refund of the amount paid. If only some items could not be supplied, you are not charged for those items, or the amount for them is refunded.

3. Refunds for online payments

Refunds for orders paid online by card or wallet are made back to the original card or wallet through PayHere, our payment gateway. We start the refund once it is approved, but the time it takes to reach your account depends on your bank or wallet provider: it is usually 5–14 working days depending on your bank. We cannot refund an online payment in cash or to a different card.

4. Cash on delivery orders

For cash on delivery orders nothing is charged until you pay the rider, so a cancelled order before handover costs you nothing. If you return items at handover, you pay only for the items you keep. If a problem is found after you have paid, approved refunds are given as cash or as Blynk credit, as agreed with you.

5. Damaged, missing, wrong or expired items

If an item is damaged, missing, wrong or past its expiry date:

  1. Report it within 24 hours of delivery on WhatsApp +94 71 710 7374.
  2. Send your order number and a clear photo of the item (and its label, packaging or expiry date where relevant).
  3. We will review it and, where the problem is confirmed, offer a replacement, a refund for that item, or Blynk credit.

Where possible, please check your order at handover and tell the rider about any problem straight away.

6. Fresh and perishable items

Fruits, vegetables, dairy, bakery, meat, fish and other perishables cannot be returned simply because you changed your mind. If a perishable item arrives in poor condition, report it within 24 hours with a photo as described above and we will make it right. Natural variation in size, colour or weight is not a defect.

7. What is not refundable

  • Items you have opened or used, unless they were faulty, wrong or expired on delivery.
  • Problems reported more than 24 hours after delivery, unless the law requires otherwise.
  • Change-of-mind returns for perishable items.

This policy does not affect your rights under Sri Lankan consumer law, including the Consumer Affairs Authority Act No. 9 of 2003.

8. Chargebacks

If you have a problem with an online payment, please contact us first: most issues are resolved faster directly. If you raise a chargeback with your bank for a payment that we have already refunded, or for an order that was delivered as ordered, we may provide the order and delivery details to the bank and PayHere, and we may restrict online payment on your account while the dispute is open.
