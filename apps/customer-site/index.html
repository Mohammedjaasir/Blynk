Questions

Good to know.

How much is delivery?

From LKR 100, depending on how far you are. The app shows the exact fee before you place the order.

How do I pay?

In cash to your rider when the order arrives.

When can I order?

Every day, from 8 AM to 9 PM.

Do you deliver to my area?

Within 4 km of the Clock Tower in Dharga Town, including Aluthgama. The app checks your pin for you.

Do I need the Play Store?

No. Open Blynk in your browser and add it to your Home Screen. It is free.
