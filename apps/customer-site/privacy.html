1. Who we are

Blynk is a quick-commerce grocery delivery service operated by [Registered business name] (business registration no. [Business registration no.]), [Registered address], Sri Lanka ("Blynk", "we", "us"). We deliver groceries and essentials from our Dharga Town store to addresses within about 4 km, including Aluthgama.

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Blynk app, the Blynk website (including the web shop) and our delivery service, why we collect it and the choices you have. For the purposes of the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka ("PDPA"), Blynk is the controller of this data.

2. The data we collect

Data you give us

  • Mobile phone number: used to create your account and to sign you in with a one-time code sent by SMS.
  • Name and, if you choose to add one, an email address.
  • Delivery addresses, including the map pin you set and any directions you add, so a rider can find you.
  • Orders: what you put in your basket, what you order, notes, coupons or points used, and your order history.
  • Payment method: whether you chose cash on delivery or online payment. Card and wallet details for online payments are entered on PayHere's secure payment page and are never received or stored by Blynk. We receive only the payment result (for example, paid or failed) and a payment reference.
  • Support messages: calls, WhatsApp messages, photos of damaged or missing items and other messages you send us.

Data collected automatically

  • Location for delivery: when you set an address, the app may use your device location (with your permission) to place the map pin. We do not track customers' live location.
  • Rider location: we collect the live location of our riders only while they are on a delivery, so you and our team can follow the order.
  • Push notification tokens: an identifier for your device so we can send order updates.
  • App, device and log information: app version, device model, operating system, IP address, error reports and records of actions in the service (for example, sign-in attempts), used to keep the service working and secure.
  • Cookies and similar technologies on the website: our website may use Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics 4, Google Ads and the Meta (Facebook) pixel to measure visits and the performance of our advertising. Where your browser sends a "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track" signal, these are set to not store analytics or advertising data.

3. How we use your data

  • To take and deliver your orders: preparing your basket, calculating the delivery fee for your address, assigning a rider and handing over the order.
  • To verify your sign-in with an SMS code and keep one account per phone number.
  • To process payments through PayHere when you pay online, and to issue refunds.
  • To support you: answering questions, handling complaints, refunds and missing or damaged items.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse and keep our customers, riders and staff safe.
  • To send service messages by SMS and push notification (for example, sign-in codes, order confirmations and delivery updates).
  • To send offers only where the law allows it or you have agreed. You can opt out at any time in the app or by contacting us; service messages will still be sent.
  • To understand and improve the service using analytics.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping accounting and tax records and responding to lawful requests.

4. Legal basis for processing

Under the PDPA we process your personal data on one or more of these grounds:

  • Contract: to provide the service you ask for, such as delivering an order.
  • Consent: for example, device location, push notifications, marketing messages and website analytics or advertising cookies where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that already took place.
  • Legal obligation: for example, keeping invoices and accounting records.
  • Legitimate interests: for example, preventing fraud, securing our systems and improving the service, balanced against your rights.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as needed:

  • Riders receive your name, phone number, delivery address and map pin for the order they are delivering, and only for that delivery.
  • SMS providers that deliver sign-in codes and order messages.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to deliver push notifications.
  • PayHere, our payment gateway, when you pay online.
  • Hosting and cloud providers that store and run our systems.
  • Analytics and advertising providers (Google and Meta) for website measurement, as described above.
  • Authorities, courts or regulators when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Blynk, our customers or others.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganised, under the same protections.

Our service providers may use your data only to provide their services to us.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers (for example, cloud hosting, Google and Meta) may store or process data outside Sri Lanka. Where this happens we take steps required by the PDPA so that your data stays protected, such as using providers with appropriate safeguards and contractual commitments.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires. As examples, to be confirmed by the owner:

  • Orders and invoices: kept for the period required for accounting and tax purposes under Sri Lankan law.
  • Account data (name, phone, addresses): kept while your account is active, and deleted or anonymised after you delete it, except where we must keep it.
  • Logs and security records: kept for a limited period, for example [number] months.
  • Rider location during deliveries: kept for a limited period, for example [number] days, for support and safety.
  • Support messages: kept while needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable time afterwards.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the Blynk app, or ask us to delete it by contacting us (see Contact us). When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data, except records we must keep by law (for example, invoices and payment records) or need to resolve an open order, refund or dispute.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you have the right, subject to the conditions and exceptions in the law, to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erase your data;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on consent;
  • object to processing, including direct marketing;
  • data portability, where applicable;
  • complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully.

To use these rights, contact us. We may need to confirm your identity (for example, with a code sent to your phone number) before acting on a request, and we will reply within the time the law requires.

10. Children

Blynk is not directed to children under 18. Accounts must be created and orders placed by adults. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls for staff, sign-in by one-time codes and keeping card details with PayHere rather than on our systems. No service is completely secure, so please keep your phone and SMS codes private and tell us at once if you think your account has been misused.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by message before they take effect.
