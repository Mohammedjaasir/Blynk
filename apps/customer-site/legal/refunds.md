If something is wrong with your Blynk order, tell us quickly and we will replace it or refund you to your Blynk Wallet. Because we deliver food and daily essentials, the time limits below are short.

### 1. Report time limits

| Problem | Report within | What we do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing or wrong item | 24 hours of delivery | Deliver the missing item or refund it |
| Damaged or leaking item | Before rider leaves, or within 2 hours | Replace or refund |
| Fresh, chilled or frozen item spoiled | 2 hours of delivery | Replace or refund |
| Expired item (past date on pack) | 24 hours of delivery | Replace or refund, and we pick it up |
| Unopened non-food item, changed mind | 3 days of delivery | Refund the item price, minus a pickup fee |
| Defective electronic or household item | 7 days of delivery | Replace, repair or refund, per supplier warranty |

To report, contact us by {{phoneLink}} or {{whatsappLink}}, or through the app, with your order number and a photo of the item.

### 2. Items we cannot take back

For health and safety reasons, the following cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged, wrong or expired:

- Opened food, drinks or personal care products
- Fresh produce, bread, dairy, chilled and frozen goods
- Opened diapers, sanitary products and baby care items
- Medicines and health products
- Mobile reloads, bill payments and DTH payments once completed
- Gift cards and vouchers

### 3. Refunds

1. All approved refunds are credited to your Blynk Wallet, whichever payment method you used.
2. Your Blynk Wallet balance can be redeemed on future orders on Blynk.
3. Approved refunds are credited within **24 hours**.
4. The delivery fee is refunded to your Blynk Wallet if the whole order was wrong, damaged or not delivered because of us.
5. Blynk Points used on a refunded item are returned to your account. Points earned on it are removed.
6. This refund method is subject to change. The policy in force when you place an order applies to that order.

### 4. Cancelling an order

1. Orders can be cancelled only before we start packing. We call you before packing, so you can cancel then.
2. Once packing has started, the order cannot be cancelled.
3. Payments for cancelled orders are refunded to your Blynk Wallet.
4. We may cancel an order if items are out of stock, the address is outside our area, or we suspect fraud. Any payment will be refunded in full to your Blynk Wallet.
5. This cancellation policy is subject to change. The policy in force when you place an order applies to that order.

### 5. Failed deliveries

If we cannot reach you or you refuse an order without a valid reason, the delivery fee will be charged. Repeated refused deliveries may lead to cash on delivery being disabled on your account.

### 6. Your legal rights

This policy does not affect your rights under the Consumer Affairs Authority Act No. 9 of 2003. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your complaint, you may contact the Consumer Affairs Authority of Sri Lanka.
