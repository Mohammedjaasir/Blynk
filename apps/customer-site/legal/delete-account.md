You can delete your Blynk account and the personal data that goes with it at any time. You do not need the app installed to ask.

### 1. Delete it in the Blynk app

1. Open the Blynk app (or blynk.lk/app) and sign in with your mobile number.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
4. Read what will be removed and tap **Delete account** again to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

### 2. Ask us to delete it

If you cannot use the app (for example you no longer have it, or you cannot receive our SMS code), send us a deletion request from the mobile number registered on your account:

- WhatsApp us: {{whatsappLink}}
- Call us: {{phoneLink}}
- Email: {{privacyEmail}} - include your registered mobile number

We confirm that the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account within **7 days** and tell you when it is done.

### 3. What is deleted

- Your name, mobile number, email and date of birth
- Your saved addresses and delivery pins
- Your app settings, notification and SMS preferences, and your phone's notification token
- Your Blynk Points and any unused coupons
- Your name and number on any upcoming dental or doctor bookings (the bookings are cancelled)

### 4. What we keep, and for how long

- **Order and payment records** - kept for up to **6 years** because tax and accounting law requires it. They are no longer linked to your name or number.
- **Blynk Wallet balance** - please use or ask us about any balance before you delete; it cannot be paid out after the account is gone.
- **Records needed for safety or fraud prevention** (for example a reported problem with a delivery) - kept only as long as needed, at most **12 months**.

Read more in our Privacy Policy.
