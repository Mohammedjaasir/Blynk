### 2. How we use it

1. To process, confirm and deliver your orders, including calling you before packing.
2. To take payment, issue refunds to your Blynk Wallet and manage Blynk Points.
   While a delivery is on its way, to show you your rider's position on the map and an estimated arrival time.
3. To process mobile reloads and bill payments you request.
4. To provide customer support and handle returns and complaints.
5. To prevent fraud and keep the Platform secure.
6. To improve our product range, app and service using usage data.
7. To send offers and updates by SMS, WhatsApp, email or app notification, **only if you agree**. You can opt out at any time.
8. To show you Blynk ads on other websites and social media (remarketing), through cookies, where you allow it.
9. To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

### 3. Legal basis

We use your data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (your orders), to meet legal obligations, for our legitimate interests in running and securing the business, or with your consent (marketing and non-essential cookies). You may withdraw consent at any time.

### 4. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share only what is needed with:

- Our delivery riders — your name, phone number and address for that order
- Payment providers and banks (for example PayHere) — to process online payments
- Doctors and clinics — your name, phone number and notes, only for an appointment you book
- Google (Firebase) — to deliver app notifications to your phone
- Reload and bill payment providers — the number and amount you submit
- Hosting, SMS, WhatsApp and IT service providers that run the Platform for us
- Analytics and advertising providers such as Google and Meta, through cookies and tags
- Government authorities, when required by law

Some providers may store data outside Sri Lanka. Where this happens, we take steps required by the PDPA to keep it protected.

### 5. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and Google Tag Manager to keep you logged in, remember your cart, measure how the Platform is used and run remarketing ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Essential features such as login and cart may stop working if you do.

### 6. How long we keep it

- Account data — while your account is active, and up to **12 months** after you close it
- Order and payment records — up to **6 years** for tax and accounting
- Call and chat records — up to **6 months**
- Appointment records — up to **12 months** after the appointment
- Seller applications — we use what you send at blynk.lk/seller only to call you about selling with Blynk, keep it for up to **12 months**, and delete it when you ask us to
- Marketing data — until you opt out

### 7. Security

We protect your data with access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited staff access. Riders see only the details needed for their delivery. No system is fully secure. If a breach is likely to harm you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Authority as the law requires.

### 8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

- Give you a copy of your personal data
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- Delete your data, where we no longer need it by law. To delete your Blynk account, use **Profile -> Delete account** in the app, or follow the steps at https://blynk.lk/delete-account
- Stop or limit certain uses, including marketing
- Withdraw consent you gave earlier

Send requests to {{privacyRequestsTo}}. We will respond within **21** working days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

### 9. Children

The Platform is not intended for children under 18 without a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children without that consent.
